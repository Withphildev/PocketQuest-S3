Keyboard & PS5 controller helpPair the controller to the browser device in its Bluetooth settings (not to StickS3). Standard browser mapping required. D-pad/left stick move Nova during exploration only, never select battle actions. Cross engages or sets out outside battle. In battle: single Cross (X) Attack; double-tap Cross within 250ms Power Strike instead of Attack. Release X between taps. Single Attack waits 250ms to distinguish a double-tap; no Attack is spent before Power Strike. Circle (○) Guard; Square (□) Run; Triangle (△) Potion each highlight and use immediately. No separate confirmation or R2 required. On-screen Attack and Power buttons remain immediate single taps. L1/R1 select the previous/next living enemy; changing target cancels a pending X tap. Run is blocked during boss fights; Power Strike needs focus, Potion needs inventory. Guard restores focus. Victories return to exploration automatically. L2 toggles fullscreen; use Exit or Escape to leave. Release controls after connecting or returning to the page. Keyboard: WASD/arrows, Space to engage. Tap an enemy or its name to select it. Music starts only after tapping Enable music; returning from background requires Resume music. Gamepad access may require HTTPS or localhost; ordinary HTTP hotspot pages may not expose it.

Nova’s journey

Head home by leaving the road’s left edge. One clear stretch takes you back to Oakwood. Earned XP stays yours; new outings start one tier below your highest road tier. You can abandon an active battle to rest immediately.

    Adventure log

      Power strike costs 1 focus. Guard halves incoming damage and restores 1 focus. Potions heal 18 HP and use a turn. The captain’s third-turn heavy strike is telegraphed.

      Original illustrated assets reused without alteration. Captain uses enlarged goblin placeholder artwork. Road battles are turn-based. Optional seeded chests hold healing or a potion; opened state is saved by StickS3. Controller fullscreen uses a landscape fallback when native fullscreen is unavailable.
